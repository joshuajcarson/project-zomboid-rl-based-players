# B4 M2a fix: server-side removal of a zombie the server ignores
- Date / time: 2026-10-30, started ~16:25 CT
- Milestone: M2a (find and fix the fake fight artifact)
- Question: does removing a still-alive zombie server-side (`zclear` at its position) after its second lethal hit make fake fights end with both zombies dead when the character really kills both?
- Hypothesis: yes; stuck episodes drop from ~90% to well under 30% and two-kill episodes rise above 40%.

## Pre-registered (fixed before running; never edited afterwards)
- Design: ONE fake session, 20 episodes of `charge:1.8:0`, fight-v4 field, seed 11 (Batch 3 used 7), 4x clock, `PZ_FAKE_FORCE_KILL=1` (env.py FAKE_FORCE_KILL; the fix applies only to the fake backend and only to a zombie that already received >= 2 lethal hits and is still in the observation). Stuck episode = steps >= 45 with >= 1 kill and a zombie alive; two-kill episode = zombies_end 0.
- SUCCESS if: >= 40% of episodes are two-kill AND < 30% are stuck (the M2a acceptance test), and zombies that died normally (first lethal hit) are untouched (no `forced` mark on a zombie with 1 lethal hit).
- FAILURE if: >= 70% of episodes are still stuck (the fix does not remove the zombie), or the session crashes.
- INCONCLUSIVE if: neither (30-70% stuck, or two-kill share between 20% and 40% with stuck < 30%); then extend to a second session before deciding.
- Stopping rule: run all 20 episodes; stop early only for an invalid run (crash, fake client not connecting).
- Report also: mean kills and damage for the session (NOT compared to the real baseline here: that is M2's next batch), number of forced removals per episode.
