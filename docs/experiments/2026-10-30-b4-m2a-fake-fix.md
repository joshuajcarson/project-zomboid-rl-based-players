# B4 M2a fix: server-side removal of a zombie the server ignores
- Date / time: 2026-10-30, started ~16:25 CT
- Milestone: M2a (find and fix the fake fight artifact)
- Question: does removing a still-alive zombie server-side (`zclear` at its position) after its second lethal hit make fake fights end with both zombies dead when the character really kills both?
- Hypothesis: yes; stuck episodes drop from ~90% to well under 30% and two-kill episodes rise above 40%.

## Pre-registered (fixed before running; never edited afterwards)
- Design: ONE fake session, 20 episodes of `charge:1.8:0`, fight-v4 field, seed 11 (Batch 3 used 7), 4x clock, `PZ_FAKE_FORCE_KILL=1` (env.py FAKE_FORCE_KILL; the fix applies only to the fake backend and only to a zombie that already received >= 2 lethal hits and is still in the observation). Stuck episode = steps >= 45 with >= 1 kill and a zombie alive; two-kill episode = zombies_end 0.
- SUCCESS if: >= 40% of episodes are two-kill AND < 30% are stuck (the M2a acceptance test), and zombies that died normally (first lethal hit) are untouched (no `forced` mark on a zombie with 1 lethal hit).
- FAILURE if: >= 70% of episodes are still stuck (the fix does not remove the zombie), or the session crashes.
- INCONCLUSIVE if: neither (30-70% stuck, or two-kill share between 20% and 40% with stuck < 30%); then extend to a second session before deciding.
- Stopping rule: run all 20 episodes; stop early only for an invalid run (crash, fake client not connecting).
- Report also: mean kills and damage for the session (NOT compared to the real baseline here: that is M2's next batch), number of forced removals per episode.

## Result (filled at batch end, 2026-10-30 16:25 CT)
- Data: `results/experiments-2026-10-30/b4_m2a_fake_fix.jsonl` (20 episodes).
- Numbers: two-kill episodes 18 of 20 (90%); stuck 2 of 20 (10%); zero-kill stalls 0. Kills 1.90 (se 0.07), damage 15.2 (se 2.7), mean steps 27.7; kills per episode {1: 2, 2: 18}. Forced removals: 17, in 10 of 20 episodes; none on a zombie with fewer than 2 lethal hits.
- Verdict: **SUCCESS** against the pre-registered criteria (>= 40% two-kill: 90%; < 30% stuck: 10%; no forced mark on a normally killed zombie: 0). The M2a acceptance test is passed.
- Unexpected learnings:
  1. After the fix the fake is back at the 10-26 refit level (kills 1.90 vs 2.0 then; damage 15.2 vs 18.9 then). So the 2.0-to-1.0 drop between the refit and Batch 2 was this artifact (server ignoring lethal hits), not a change in combat realism: the dropped kills were bookkeeping, not combat.
  2. The server ignored lethal hits in about half of the episodes in this run (forced removals in 10 of 20), not in 90% as in Batch 3: the incidence varies by session/seed, which is part of why the two Batch 2 sessions differed.
  3. The fake is now clearly too EASY relative to the real baseline: kills 1.90 vs 1.36 (+0.54) and damage 15.2 vs 39.2 (-24), the direction predicted before Batch 2. The remaining gap is combat difficulty (zombie damage, deaths, stalls), which is the calibration question for M2.
- Validity notes: single session (seed 11, n=20); the comparison with the real baseline here is informal (not pre-registered; M2's next batch does it properly). Fix is now default ON (`PZ_FAKE_FORCE_KILL=0` disables).
- MILESTONE IMPACT: M2a done. M2 unblocked: the target is now a calibration of the fake's difficulty (bite damage / stagger / hit model) so that kills and damage match the real baseline within the M2 bands.
- Follow-ups / next experiment: Batch 5 = M2 calibration sweep (bite damage scale arms, interleaved in one fake session).
