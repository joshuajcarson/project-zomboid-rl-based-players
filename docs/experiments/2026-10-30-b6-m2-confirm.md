# B6 M2 confirmation: fake at bite 6.0 vs the real baseline
- Date / time: 2026-10-30, started ~16:45 CT
- Milestone: M2 (fake-vs-real fidelity for the solo fight)
- Question: with the force-kill fix and bite damage 6.0, does the fake match the real baseline (kills 1.36, damage 39.2) within the M2 bands over two fresh sessions?
- Hypothesis: yes for damage; kills may sit near the upper band edge (Batch 5: 1.65, se 0.13).

## Pre-registered (fixed before running; never edited afterwards)
- Real target (Batch 1 pooled n=69): kills 1.36 (95% CI 1.17-1.56), damage 39.2 (32.3-46.2).
- Design: two independent sessions (full cleanup and fresh server between them), each 40 episodes of `charge:1.8:0` on the fake backend at 4x clock, fight-v4 field, force-kill fix on, `PZ_BITE_ARMS=6.0` (bite damage 6.0 for every episode), seeds 7 and 11. Runner `scripts/_b6_m2.sh`. Real episodes = steps >= 5; pooled n >= 60.
- SUCCESS (M2 acceptance) if: pooled mean kills within 0.3 of 1.36 (1.06-1.66) AND pooled mean damage within 10 of 39.2 (29.2-49.2), AND neither session alone is outside the failure band below.
- FAILURE if: pooled kills differ from 1.36 by more than 0.5 (outside 0.86-1.86) OR pooled damage differs from 39.2 by more than 20 (outside 19.2-59.2), OR either session alone is outside those bands.
- INCONCLUSIVE if: neither (grey zone, e.g. kills 1.67-1.86 or damage 49.3-59.2): state the gap with intervals and decide on one more bite value (4.5-7.5) in a follow-up sweep with more episodes per arm.
- Stopping rule: all requested episodes per session; stop early only for an invalid run (server crash, fake client not connecting). No stopping on apparent effects.
- Also report: death share (damage >= 80) and stuck share per session vs the real (S1 26%, S2 8% deaths; 21% stalls in S2), and median damage.

## Result (filled at batch end, 2026-10-30 16:57 CT)
- Data: `results/experiments-2026-10-30/b6_m2_fake_s1.jsonl`, `b6_m2_fake_s2.jsonl` (40 real episodes each, no aborted resets).
- Numbers (fake, bite 6.0, force-kill on, charge:1.8:0):
  - S1: kills 1.82 (se 0.06), damage 44.5 (se 4.7), median 40.8, 7 deaths (>= 80 dmg), 2 stuck; kills {1: 7, 2: 33}.
  - S2: kills 1.68 (se 0.09), damage 51.5 (se 5.0), median 49.1, 14 deaths, 1 stuck; kills {0: 2, 1: 9, 2: 29}.
  - Pooled n=80: kills 1.75 (95% CI 1.64-1.86), damage 48.0 (41.2-54.7), 21 deaths (26%).
  - Gap fake - real (1.36 / 39.2): kills +0.39 (se 0.11, z 3.4), damage +8.8 (se 4.9, z 1.8).
- Verdict: **INCONCLUSIVE** against the pre-registered criteria: pooled damage is inside the success band (48.0 in 29.2-49.2) but pooled kills are not (1.75 > 1.66), and neither the pooled numbers nor either session crosses the failure bands (kills 0.86-1.86, damage 19.2-59.2). It is exactly the grey-zone example written in the card (kills 1.67-1.86). M2's acceptance is NOT met.
- Unexpected learnings:
  1. Batch 5's marginal pass at bite 6.0 (kills 1.65, damage 40.8, n=20) was partly luck: the larger sample moves both metrics up (kills 1.75, damage 48.0). The sweep's per-arm n=20 cannot resolve 0.1-0.3 kill differences; selecting the arm that happened to pass was selection on noise.
  2. Damage and kills do not move together under this knob: bite scaling fixes damage and deaths (fake 26% deaths = real S1's 26%, above the real pooled 16%) but leaves kills too high; the real client loses kills to stalls (21% of S2 episodes) and floor-hit misses, mechanisms the fake lacks (fake stuck share is 1-2 of 40).
  3. The two fake sessions differ more than their se suggests on damage (44.5 vs 51.5) and deaths (7 vs 14): sessions still add variance beyond episode noise on the fake too.
- Validity notes: both sessions complete, no invariant issues, force-kill fix on (stuck 2 and 1 of 40), seeds 7 and 11.
- MILESTONE IMPACT: modify M2 next step: add a second knob (swing hit probability scale) to lower kills, sweep (bite x hit scale) with a candidate then confirmed on fresh sessions; keep the acceptance test unchanged.
- Follow-ups / next experiment: Batch 7 = M2 two-knob sweep (bite x hit scale, interleaved arms in one session).
