# H-L1 Does a lessons file make the planner better at its goals?
- Date / time: 2026-11-15, pre-registered ~07:45 CT (before any H-L1 run)
- Milestone: H-L1 (planner learns from experience: lessons file), design in `docs/planner-learning.md`
- Question: after a training phase in which the planner rewrites a short lessons list after every episode (Reflexion-style), does the same planner with those lessons frozen into its prompt (`llmL`) do better than the plain planner (`llm`) on the fixed scenario?
- Background: on the fixed scenario (HB-F sessions 1-2) the plain planner trails the rule planner on end hp (62 vs 79) and deaths (0.17 vs 0.08) while beating it on needs (0.16 vs 0.30); its wasted loots are already low (0.25 per episode). The lessons have room mostly in fighting and disengaging, less in looting.
- Hypothesis: llmL ends with more health than llm (fewer deaths) without worse needs.

## Pre-registered (fixed before running; never edited afterwards)
- Scenario: the fixed scenario exactly as HB-F (`hybrid_run.py --fixes`, supplies Base.Apple:3,Base.WaterBottle:2,Base.Bandage:2 at 10753,9407, box 10740 9385 10770 9400, hunger and thirst 0.6, 2 zombies on the 8-14 ring, 120 s episodes, Sonnet).
- Phase 1, training (exploratory, not judged): `--arms llmL --lessons results/experiments-2026-11-15/hl1_lessons.json --minutes 60 --seed 53 --out results/experiments-2026-11-15/hl1_train.jsonl`. After every episode `hybrid.reflect` rewrites the list (max 8 lessons, under 25 words each, tagged general/scenario). The learning curve (end hp and deaths by episode) is reported, not judged.
- Freeze: after training, the lessons file is copied to `hl1_lessons_frozen.json`; its sha256 goes into the run log before evaluation starts. No edits by hand.
- Phase 2, evaluation (judged): `--arms llm,llmL --lessons results/experiments-2026-11-15/hl1_lessons_frozen.json --freeze --minutes 110`, two sessions on fresh servers with seeds 59 and 61 (`hl1_eval_s1.jsonl`, `hl1_eval_s2.jsonl`). Target >= 30 episodes per arm pooled; if short, a third session (seed 67) runs for the sample target alone.
- Watchdog on every run: `--arms <arms> --hunger 0.6 --thirst 0.6 --supplies --supplies-at 10753,9407`.
- Analysis: `python scripts/hl_analyze.py --a llm --b llmL hl1_eval_s1.jsonl hl1_eval_s2.jsonl` (pooled). Metrics: end hp (deaths = 0), deaths, needs (survivors), wasted loots per episode (took nothing, or started while carrying food, water and bandages).
- SUCCESS if (llmL - llm end hp z >= 2 AND llmL needs <= llm needs + 0.10) OR (llmL - llm wasted loots z <= -2 AND end hp z > -1 AND needs <= llm + 0.10).
- FAILURE if llmL - llm end hp z <= -1, OR llmL needs > llm needs + 0.15, OR llmL wasted loots z >= 2.
- INCONCLUSIVE otherwise.
- Stopping rule: each run lasts its minutes; stop early only on a scenario watchdog alert (start state, stray, immobile, step-1, stall, idle), a crash or a stall; a stopped evaluation session is rerun from scratch with the same frozen file.
- Division of labour (to record at the end): which behaviours the lessons changed and whether they are decisions the planner should own or behaviour a real-time skill should take over.
