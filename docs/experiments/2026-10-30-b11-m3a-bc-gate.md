# B11 M3a gate A: the existing behaviour-cloned fighter vs the scripted fighter (on the calibrated fake)
- Date / time: 2026-10-30, started ~18:45 CT
- Milestone: M3a (imitation gate); gate B (real client) is a later batch
- Question: is `models/fight_bc3.pt` (behaviour cloning of the scripted fighter, trained on 2026-10-26, sha1 prefix e624cb83a0, never benchmarked in the ledger) non-inferior to `charge:1.8:0` on the calibrated fake?
- Hypothesis: yes or nearly: the scripted fighter is a deterministic function of the observation and the checkpoint imitates it; differences come from the discretised action choice near the swing-distance threshold.

## Pre-registered (fixed before running; never edited afterwards)
- Candidate: exactly one checkpoint, `models/fight_bc3.pt` (sha1 prefix e624cb83a0), greedy. No other checkpoint is evaluated in this batch.
- Design: ONE fake session of 120 episodes interleaved by episode between `models/fight_bc3.pt` and `charge:1.8:0` (60 each), fight-v4 field, seed 7, 4x clock, calibrated fake fight profile (bite 2.25 / hit scale 0.6), force-kill on. `python scripts/compare_arms.py <file> --by policy`. Real episodes = steps >= 5; need >= 55 per arm.
- SUCCESS (gate A passed) if: learned mean kills >= scripted mean kills - 0.2 AND learned mean damage <= scripted mean damage + 8.
- FAILURE if: learned kills < scripted kills - 0.5 OR learned damage > scripted damage + 15.
- INCONCLUSIVE if: neither.
- Stopping rule: all 120 episodes; stop early only for an invalid run (server crash, fake client not connecting, checkpoint fails to load).
- Also report: kills/damage/deaths/stuck per arm with z for the difference; action choice differences if cheap.

## Result (filled at batch end, 2026-10-30 18:57 CT)
- Data: `results/experiments-2026-10-30/b11_m3a_bc_gate.jsonl` (120 episodes, 60 per arm, all real).
- Numbers (calibrated fake, interleaved, seed 7):
  - `charge:1.8:0`: kills 1.67 (se 0.06), damage 37.1 (se 3.0), kills {1: 20, 2: 40}, 19 stuck, 1 death, median damage 35.4, mean steps 37.1, 31.5 attack actions per episode.
  - `models/fight_bc3.pt`: kills 1.57 (se 0.07), damage 37.1 (se 2.8), kills {0: 2, 1: 22, 2: 36}, 22 stuck, 2 deaths, median damage 39.4, mean steps 37.2, 31.2 attack actions per episode.
  - Difference BC - scripted: kills -0.10 (se 0.10, z -1.05; 95% CI -0.30 to +0.10), damage -0.07 (se 4.16, z -0.02).
- Verdict: **SUCCESS** against the pre-registered criteria (gate A): kills 1.57 >= 1.67 - 0.2 = 1.47, damage 37.1 <= 37.1 + 8. Caveat recorded: the 95% interval of the kills difference includes values below the -0.2 margin, so the criterion (written on means) is met but statistical non-inferiority at n=60 per arm is not established; gate B pools two sessions on the real client.
- Unexpected learnings:
  1. The BC checkpoint is a near copy of the scripted policy (same attack count per episode, same episode length, damage identical): the imitation works, and its only visible difference is 2 zero-kill episodes (kills {0: 2}) against none for the scripted arm.
  2. In this session the scripted arm's kills (1.67) sit just above the M2 band edge (1.66), higher than Batch 9's 1.59: fake sessions still differ by ~0.1 kills at the same settings.
  3. The calibrated fake runs 120 episodes in ~13 minutes; a real-client comparison of the same size takes ~100 minutes.
- Validity notes: valid run: both arms interleaved in one session, all 120 episodes real, no aborted resets, checkpoint loaded without conversion (25-64-64-10).
- MILESTONE IMPACT: M3a gate A passed on the fake; gate B (real client, >= 55 real episodes per arm pooled over 2 sessions, same criteria) is the next step. Learned policies remain unvalidated on the real client until then.
