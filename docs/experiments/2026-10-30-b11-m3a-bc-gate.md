# B11 M3a gate A: the existing behaviour-cloned fighter vs the scripted fighter (on the calibrated fake)
- Date / time: 2026-10-30, started ~18:45 CT
- Milestone: M3a (imitation gate); gate B (real client) is a later batch
- Question: is `models/fight_bc3.pt` (behaviour cloning of the scripted fighter, trained on 2026-10-26, sha1 prefix e624cb83a0, never benchmarked in the ledger) non-inferior to `charge:1.8:0` on the calibrated fake?
- Hypothesis: yes or nearly: the scripted fighter is a deterministic function of the observation and the checkpoint imitates it; differences come from the discretised action choice near the swing-distance threshold.

## Pre-registered (fixed before running; never edited afterwards)
- Candidate: exactly one checkpoint, `models/fight_bc3.pt` (sha1 prefix e624cb83a0), greedy. No other checkpoint is evaluated in this batch.
- Design: ONE fake session of 120 episodes interleaved by episode between `models/fight_bc3.pt` and `charge:1.8:0` (60 each), fight-v4 field, seed 7, 4x clock, calibrated fake fight profile (bite 2.25 / hit scale 0.6), force-kill on. `python scripts/compare_arms.py <file> --by policy`. Real episodes = steps >= 5; need >= 55 per arm.
- SUCCESS (gate A passed) if: learned mean kills >= scripted mean kills - 0.2 AND learned mean damage <= scripted mean damage + 8.
- FAILURE if: learned kills < scripted kills - 0.5 OR learned damage > scripted damage + 15.
- INCONCLUSIVE if: neither.
- Stopping rule: all 120 episodes; stop early only for an invalid run (server crash, fake client not connecting, checkpoint fails to load).
- Also report: kills/damage/deaths/stuck per arm with z for the difference; action choice differences if cheap.
